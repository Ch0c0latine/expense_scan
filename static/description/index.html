Expense Receipt Scanner

A local AI reads your receipts.
Photograph one, get a filled-in expense.

The reading happens on your own Odoo server. A small neural network (PP-OCR, run by ONNX Runtime) reads the text, and a parser written for this module turns it into an expense: merchant, date, total, currency, tax, category. No API key, no account, no fee per scan, and no receipt ever leaves your server.

A restaurant receipt: merchant, date, time, total and 19 % VAT read, category "Meals" recognised, the expense checked against the company rules.

Made to work out of the box

  • One module, open source (LGPL-3). It installs on its own, on Odoo Community, and it does not depend on any outside service, paid or free.
  • Local AI, small footprint. The OCR models weigh 47 MB in total. The parser is plain code, not a language model: it was written and tuned on more than 1,500 real receipts, French and from fifteen countries, and checked against known amounts.
  • It tells you what it is not sure about. A doubtful field carries a hint under it, so you check one field rather than the whole receipt. The module never approves an expense by itself.
  • Eleven languages for the interface: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian and Danish.

How it works

1. Scan

The Scan button of the expense list opens the camera on a phone (or the gallery, or your files). On a computer, upload a photo or a PDF.

2. Read

The receipt is found in the photo, cropped and straightened, then read: merchant, date, time, total, currency and tax.

3. Review

The form opens at once, receipt on one side, fields on the other. A hint shows under each field to check, and goes away once it is corrected.

Around the scan

  • Category recognition from the receipt words, the printed activity code, the price per litre or kWh, known brands, and the merchants your team already filed. The module creates no category of its own: every day it learns the words found on the receipts your team filed in each of yours.
  • Receipts from abroad: dates, totals and currencies of European, Nordic, Swiss and US receipts. The expense is recorded in the currency of the receipt; a tax paid abroad is not carried as deductible tax.
  • Exchange rates: the reference rates of the European Central Bank are added every day, so that a foreign receipt is converted at the rate of its own day.
  • Your chart of accounts: the tax printed on the receipt goes to the journal entry, with the tax of its rate, even on receipts with several rates.
  • Receipts in several pieces (till receipt and card slip) are merged into one expense, or split when they describe two purchases.
  • Manual retouch: rotate and crop in the browser, or accept the automatic suggestion.
  • Projects: re-invoice an expense to a project, or charge it to the project budget only; the analytic account follows the project. Approved expenses add up on one line of the sales order (with the Sales app), and an expense budget can be followed on the project.
  • Expense sheets: PDF summary with tax by rate and numbered receipts, Excel export on your own workbook.
  • Expense rules: ceilings per meal, per day or per night, words to watch. A warning shows on the expense, with a sign in the list: ⚠ for a breach, ℹ︎ for a point to check. A justification puts the sign out; the manager reads it when approving.
  • Team entry: managers enter expenses for their team members.
  • E-mail: receipts sent from an employee's private address are recognised; the subject becomes the description.
  • Phone first: receipt preview on phones, split view from 768 px.

Settings

At the end of the settings, Help and feedback opens a pre-filled page to report a bug, suggest an idea or ask for a custom adaptation. Nothing is sent from your server.

Server requirements, and what they weigh

It is a lot of names, but not a lot of weight. Everything the module adds to the server, measured on our test installation:

No GPU, no extra service, no extra daemon. Tesseract is optional, as a fallback engine.

pip install opencv-python-headless rapidocr onnxruntime pdf2image openpyxl
sudo apt install poppler-utils

The Python packages and pdftoppm are declared in the manifest: if one is missing, Odoo names it when you install the module instead of leaving a module that cannot read a receipt. With Odoo workers, set MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2 in the service environment: it stops Odoo from recycling a worker after each scan because of address space the OCR threads reserve.

This module needs packages that Odoo Online does not let you install: it is meant for Odoo you host yourself, or on Odoo.sh.

How fast, and how heavy

The whole path for one receipt: crop, straighten, read, parse, write the expense. 40 real photos of receipts from six countries, replayed three times with a different number of OCR threads. Machine: AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D (8 cores), Odoo 19 and PostgreSQL on the same computer.

  • More threads shorten the wait for the person and spend more CPU: on a busy server, set one or two threads in the settings.
  • Memory is that of the whole Odoo process after the 120 scans of the test, which started at 180 MB. The OCR engine itself adds about 160 MB once loaded.
  • The first scan after a restart adds one to three seconds (the models load). The button Test and preload the engine does it in advance, and tells you how long a scan takes on your own server.
  • A scan runs in the background of the form: the person keeps typing while the fields fill in.

Times depend on the processor and on the size of the photo. On a modest server expect longer times than these; the test button gives the exact figure for yours.

Network access, all of it

  • The OCR models (47.5 MB, data files and no code) are downloaded once, at the first scan or with the Test and preload the engine button. To avoid any download, copy them into the folder named in the settings.
  • Every day, the public reference rates of the European Central Bank are downloaded (a public file; nothing is sent). The option is on by default and has one switch in the settings.
  • Optional: the public French Sirene register can be imported, by hand, to find the activity of a merchant from its SIRET number.
  • Receipts, texts and amounts never leave the server. The module needs no activation key and keeps no data of yours anywhere else.
