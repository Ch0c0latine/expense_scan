Receipt Scanner for Expenses

Photograph a receipt, get a filled-in expense.
The OCR runs on your own server: no IAP credits, no API key, no subscription.

How it works

1. Scan

The Scan button of the expense list opens the camera on a phone (or the gallery, or your files). On a computer, upload a photo or a PDF.

2. Read

The receipt is found in the photo, cropped and straightened, then read: merchant, date, time, total, currency and tax.

3. Review

The form opens at once, receipt on one side, fields on the other. A hint shows under each field to check, and goes away once it is corrected.

Your receipts stay with you

The module uses RapidOCR (PP-OCR networks run by ONNX Runtime), or Tesseract as a fallback, installed next to Odoo. No document is sent to a third party, nothing is billed per page, and scanning works without Internet access. About one second per receipt on an ordinary server processor.

Around the scan

  • Category recognition from the receipt words, the printed activity code, the price per litre or kWh, known brands, and the merchants your team already filed.
  • Receipts from abroad: dates, totals and currencies of European, Nordic, Swiss and US receipts. The expense is recorded in the currency of the receipt; a tax paid abroad (MwSt, IVA, Moms... depending on your country) is not carried as deductible tax.
  • Exchange rates: the reference rates of the European Central Bank are added every day, so that a foreign receipt is converted at the rate of its own day. Odoo Community updates no rate on its own.
  • Your chart of accounts: the tax printed on the receipt goes to the journal entry, with the tax of its rate, even on receipts with several rates.
  • Receipts in several pieces (till receipt and card slip) are merged into one expense, or split when they describe two purchases.
  • Manual retouch: rotate and crop in the browser, or accept the automatic suggestion.
  • Projects: re-invoice an expense to a project, or charge it to the project budget only; the analytic account follows the project.
  • Expense sheets: PDF summary with tax by rate and numbered receipts, Excel export on your own workbook.
  • Expense rules: ceilings per meal, per day or per night, words to watch. A warning shows on the expense, with a sign in the list: ⚠ for a breach, ℹ︎ for a point to check. "Done" or a justification puts the sign out; the manager reads the justification when approving.
  • Team entry: managers enter expenses for their team members.
  • E-mail: receipts sent from an employee's private address are recognised; the subject becomes the description.
  • Mobile first: receipt preview on phones, split view from 768 px.

The module never approves an expense by itself: it fills in, flags what is doubtful, and leaves the decision to the employee.

Languages

English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian and Danish.

Installation

Install the Python packages in Odoo's environment:

pip install rapidocr onnxruntime opencv-python-headless pdf2image openpyxl

And the poppler-utils system package, for PDF receipts. These dependencies are declared: Odoo names a missing one when the module is installed. Tesseract stays an optional fallback engine. The Test and preload the engine button checks the installation.

With Odoo workers, set MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2 in the service environment, so that the memory reserved by the OCR threads does not make workers restart after each scan. The README gives the details.
